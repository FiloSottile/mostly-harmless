# Geomys Trust and Transparency SRL

Geomys Trust and Transparency SRL is an affiliate company of
[Geomys](https://geomys.org) which operates services related
to online trust and transparency logs.

We operate

- the [Tuscolo](https://tuscolo.sunlight.geomys.org/) and
  [Trastevere](https://trastevere.sunlight.geomys.org/)
  Certificate Transparency logs;

- commercial [transparency log witnesses](https://c2sp.org/tlog-witness)
  with SLAs;

- a public tlog witness as part of [the public witness network](https://witness-network.org/);

- the Magnolia Merkle Tree Certificates CA.
